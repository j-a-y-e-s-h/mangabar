> MangaBar is 100% Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) released under the permissive MIT License. There are no subscriptions, no paywalls, no premium tiers, and no ads.

## Free Community Edition ($0 / Forever)
- **Price:** $0.00 (Free Forever)
- **License:** MIT License (Open Source)
- **Account Requirement:** None (Zero registration, no login, no credit card required)
- **Telemetry & Tracking:** Zero (100% local, no analytics, no user tracking)
- **Storage:** Local file system only (`~/.mangabar`)
- **Features Included:**
  - Unlimited reading from 350+ community manga sources (Keiyoushi catalog)
  - Unlimited offline chapter downloads
  - Dual-sidebar docking (Left Activity Bar or Right Auxiliary Bar)
  - Full-screen & full-editor tab reader (`Ctrl+Alt+M`)
  - Event-isolated smooth zoom & pan (50% to 400%)
  - Compatibility with all modern IDEs: VS Code, Cursor, Windsurf, VSCodium
  - Local manga library & reading history tracker
